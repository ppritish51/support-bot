# Workspaces, Members & Roles

## How do I create a workspace or a sandbox?
Settings → Workspaces → "Create workspace". Choose Production or Sandbox. Sandbox
workspaces are isolated for testing — separate data and API keys, no real billing or
customer-facing emails. You can switch workspaces from the top-left workspace picker.

## What roles exist and what can they do?
There are four roles: Owner (full control incl. billing and deletion), Admin (manage
members, settings, integrations), Member (use the product), and Billing Admin (invoices
and payment methods only). Roles are set per member in Settings → Members.

## How do I invite or remove members?
Settings → Members → "Invite" and enter their email and role; they receive an email invite.
To remove someone, open their row and click "Remove" — this frees the seat at the end of
the cycle and immediately revokes their access. Removing a member does not delete content
they created.

## Can one person belong to multiple workspaces?
Yes. A user can be a member of many workspaces with a different role in each, and switch
between them with the workspace picker without logging out. Invites are per workspace.

## Who can change billing settings?
Only the Owner and members with the Billing Admin role can view invoices, change the plan,
or update payment methods. Regular Admins manage product settings and members but cannot
see billing.
