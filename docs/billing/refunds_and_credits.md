# Refunds & Credits

## What is your refund policy?
Monthly plans are non-refundable for the current period but you can cancel anytime to stop
future charges. Annual plans are eligible for a prorated refund within 30 days of the
renewal charge. Refunds are reviewed by the billing team and, when approved, returned to
the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

## How do account credits work?
Credits (from downgrades, removed seats, or goodwill adjustments) are applied
automatically to your next invoice before your card is charged. Your current credit
balance is shown in Settings → Billing → Overview. Credits never expire while the account
is active.

## I was charged twice — what should I do?
Duplicate charges are almost always a temporary authorization hold that drops off in a few
business days, not a real second charge. Check Settings → Billing → Invoices: if only one
invoice exists, the extra line on your card statement is a hold. If two invoices exist,
contact billing support and a human will investigate and issue a correction.

## Can I get a refund after cancelling?
Cancelling stops future renewals but does not automatically refund the current period.
Monthly periods already paid are not refunded; annual plans may qualify for a prorated
refund under the 30-day policy above. Refund requests are handled case by case by the
billing team.
