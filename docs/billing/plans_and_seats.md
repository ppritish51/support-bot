# Plans & Seats

## What plans are available and how do they differ?
There are three plans: Starter, Growth, and Enterprise. They differ mainly in included
seats, API rate limits (60 / 600 / custom requests per minute), data retention, and
support SLA. A full feature-by-plan comparison is on the Pricing page and in Settings →
Billing → Plan.

## How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Settings → Billing → Plan → "Change plan". Upgrades take effect immediately and are
prorated for the remainder of the current cycle. Downgrades take effect at the start of
your next billing cycle so you keep paid features until then.

## How does adding or removing seats work?
Settings → Billing → Plan → "Manage seats". Adding a seat is prorated and charged
immediately. Removing a seat credits the unused portion to your next invoice; the seat
stays usable until the end of the current cycle.

## What happens to my data if I downgrade?
Your data is retained, but features exclusive to the higher plan become read-only or
hidden. For example, if a downgrade puts you below your current data-retention window,
older data becomes inaccessible until you upgrade again — it is not deleted during the
grace period.

## Do you offer annual billing, and is there a discount?
Yes. Switching from monthly to annual (Settings → Billing → Plan) applies a two-months-
free discount (roughly 17% off) and issues a single annual invoice. Enterprise pricing is
custom and quoted by the sales team.
