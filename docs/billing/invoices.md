# Invoices

## How do I download past invoices?
Settings → Billing → Invoices lists every invoice. Click any row to view it, or use
"Download PDF". Invoices remain available for the life of the account. Account owners and
users with the Billing Admin role can access them.

## Why did my invoice amount change this month?
The three most common reasons are: (1) you added or removed seats, (2) your usage crossed
into a higher tier, or (3) a promotional discount expired. Each invoice's line items show
the exact breakdown, including proration for mid-cycle changes.

## When are invoices issued and emailed?
Invoices are generated at the moment of each charge and emailed to the account owner and
any Billing Admins within a few minutes. Annual plans are invoiced once per year on the
renewal date; monthly plans on the monthly billing date.

## Can I add a PO number, VAT ID, or custom billing details to invoices?
Yes. Settings → Billing → Billing Details lets you set your legal entity name, address,
VAT/GST/Tax ID, and an optional PO number. These fields appear on every future invoice.
Existing invoices are not retroactively changed.

## Who receives invoice and billing emails?
The account owner always receives them. You can add more recipients under Settings →
Billing → Billing Details → "Additional billing contacts". These recipients get invoices
and payment-failure notices but do not need a paid seat.
