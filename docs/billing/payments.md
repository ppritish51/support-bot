# Payments & Payment Methods

## How do I update the credit card on my account?
Go to Settings → Billing → Payment Methods and click "Update card". Enter the new card
details and click Save. The new card becomes the default for all future charges. Changes
take effect immediately; no restart is required.

## My payment failed — what happens next?
When a charge fails, we automatically retry it up to 4 times over 7 days (day 1, 3, 5,
and 7). During this dunning window your account stays active. If all retries fail, the
account is downgraded to read-only until a working payment method is added. You'll get an
email after each failed attempt.

## Which payment methods are supported?
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and Discover. Enterprise accounts can also
pay by ACH bank transfer or wire against an annual invoice — contact your account manager
to enable invoice billing.

## Can I add a backup payment method?
Yes. In Settings → Billing → Payment Methods you can add multiple cards and mark one as
default. If the default card fails, we automatically try the backup before entering the
dunning retry cycle.

## Why was my card declined even though it works elsewhere?
The most common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, or the bank blocking a
recurring/international charge. Ask your bank to allow charges from our billing descriptor,
then retry from Settings → Billing → "Retry payment".
